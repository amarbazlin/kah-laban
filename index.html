Kunafa & Dubai Chocolate FAQs

Everything you need to know about ordering the best desserts in Colombo, Sri Lanka.

Where can I buy kunafa in Colombo, Sri Lanka?

You can buy fresh kunafa in Colombo directly from KAH Laban. Our Dubai Kunafa Melt and Dubai Pista Rocket are made fresh daily with pistachio and chocolate. Order for delivery via UberEats or contact us for pickup in Colombo.

What is Dubai chocolate kunafa?

Dubai chocolate kunafa is a Middle Eastern dessert made with crispy kunafa pastry layered with pistachio cream and Belgian-style chocolate. At KAH Laban in Sri Lanka we serve it as the Dubai Kunafa Melt cup and the crispy Dubai Pista Rocket pastry.

Does KAH Laban deliver desserts in Colombo?

Yes. KAH Laban delivers desserts across Colombo. Order online through UberEats for quick dessert delivery in Colombo, or call +94761601600 and WhatsApp us for pickup and bulk orders.

Can I order KAH Laban desserts for events or bulk orders?

Yes. We offer wholesale desserts for events in Sri Lanka, including weddings, corporate functions and parties. Contact us at kahlaban160@gmail.com or +94761601600 with your date and quantity and we will prepare a custom dessert catering quote for Colombo.

Where is KAH Laban located?

KAH Laban is a dessert shop based in Colombo, Sri Lanka, serving Middle Eastern sweets and Egyptian desserts across the island. Message us on Instagram @kah.laban or call +94761601600 for the current store location and opening hours.
